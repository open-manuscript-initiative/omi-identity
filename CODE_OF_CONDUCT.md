# Code of Conduct

The Open Manuscript Initiative is committed to a professional, respectful, and inclusive collaboration environment.

Contributors are expected to discuss technical work on its merits, respect differing experience and perspectives, avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct, protect confidential/security-sensitive information, and assume good faith while remaining precise about technical risk.

Unacceptable behavior includes personal attacks, harassment, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, discriminatory language, and publishing security-sensitive information in public channels after being asked to use a private reporting path.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions or comments that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.

For security-sensitive matters, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
